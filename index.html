A few good questions

Know more.
Decide with confidence.

Is the car I’m looking at still available?

Please contact us with the stock number before arranging travel. We will confirm the car’s current status and answer questions about that specific vehicle.

Can I request more photos or a walk-around video?

Yes. Choose “Request a walk-around video” and tell us what you want to see: a cold start, the interior, undercarriage, or a particular detail. We can discuss what is available for your chosen car.

Can I arrange a visit or an independent inspection?

Call before visiting to discuss a suitable time, the vehicle’s location, and any independent inspection you would like to arrange.

What should I confirm before buying?

Ask for the vehicle’s current condition, title information, documentation, odometer status, and a written itemized price. Discuss any transport arrangements and costs before making a decision.
