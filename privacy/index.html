Privacy information

Updated September 4, 2026

This page describes the information used by the current Framingham Classic Autos website.

Contacting us

The vehicle request and question forms ask for your name, phone number, email, selected vehicle, question, and permission to contact you. The main form also asks for a purchase preference and whether you may need delivery. When submitted, these details are sent to our inquiry service and forwarded to the dealership through Telegram to respond to your request. A confirmation is shown only after the service confirms delivery.

When you send an email or call, the contact details and information you provide are used to discuss your inquiry. Please do not send payment card details, identity documents, or other sensitive records through this form.

Campaign information

The site may keep advertising link parameters in session storage on your device so that your inquiry includes the vehicle and referring campaign. This session information does not contain the name, email address, phone number, or message you type in the form. Blocking storage will not prevent browsing or contacting us.

Advertising measurement

This site uses Meta Pixels to measure page visits and understand advertising performance. Meta may receive information such as the page URL, browser and device details, IP address, and identifiers stored by Meta. The pixels do not receive the name, email address, phone number, or message you enter in our forms from this website. You can limit this activity through your browser settings and your Meta advertising preferences.

External services

Google Maps links open Google’s website. Telephone and email links open applications on your device. Those services have their own privacy practices. This build does not load a third-party live chat.

Questions

For questions about an inquiry you have sent, email sales@framinghaautos.com or call +1 (508) 204-5456.
